3. Known Graphical Fixes & Emulation Patches

Uneven Pixel Shimmer on Smooth Scrolling

The Issue: Stretching a 240x160 resolution screen to modern phone aspect ratios causes jagged pixel crawling during camera pans.

The Fix: Enable "Integer Scaling" (5x or 6x) in mGBA Video settings. Combine with the "LCD3x" shader for authentic handheld pixel grid warmth.

Audio Sample Interpolation Distortion

The Issue: The GBA DirectSound engine can produce harsh buzzing on bass notes through mobile speakers.

The Fix: Set Audio Resampler to "Sinc (High Quality)" at 44.1kHz with a 16ms buffer in mGBA Audio settings.

4. Touch Controls vs. Bluetooth Gamepads

Navigating intense action sequences and camera panning in Dragon Drive (Cezar) via capacitive touch controls works reliably when using a customized on-screen layout. Placing the virtual thumbstick slightly closer to the lower screen edge and scaling action buttons to 110% ensures comfortable reach without obstructing on-screen visual hazards.

For extended gaming sessions and demanding boss encounters, snapping your smartphone into a USB-C telescopic gamepad (such as the Razer Kishi or GameSir G8) or syncing a Bluetooth PlayStation DualSense controller provides authentic tactile feedback.
