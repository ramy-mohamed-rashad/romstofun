3. Known Graphical Fixes & Emulation Patches

Polygon Edge Seams & Texture Gaps

The Issue: Upscaling 3D models on dual-screen setups can reveal tiny single-pixel gaps along character joint seams.

The Fix: Enable "High-Resolution 3D Rendering" in DraStic and ensure Frame Skip is set strictly to "None (0)".

Touchscreen Deadzone & Stylus Input Drift

The Issue: Capacitive finger touch can feel imprecise for quick mini-game stylus taps.

The Fix: Set Touch Screen Deadzone to "Minimal" and calibrate Stylus input to 1:1 hardware touch points in DraStic settings.

4. Touch Controls vs. Bluetooth Gamepads

Navigating intense action sequences and camera panning in Spider-Man - Web Of Shadows via capacitive touch controls works reliably when using a customized on-screen layout. Placing the virtual thumbstick slightly closer to the lower screen edge and scaling action buttons to 110% ensures comfortable reach without obstructing on-screen visual hazards.

For extended gaming sessions and demanding boss encounters, snapping your smartphone into a USB-C telescopic gamepad (such as the Razer Kishi or GameSir G8) or syncing a Bluetooth PlayStation DualSense controller provides authentic tactile feedback. Physical analog sticks and textured shoulder bumpers eliminate thumb fatigue and grant flawless precision control.
