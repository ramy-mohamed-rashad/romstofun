3. Known Graphical Fixes & Emulation Patches

Polygon Edge Seams & Texture Gaps

The Issue: Upscaling 3D models on dual-screen setups can reveal tiny single-pixel gaps along character joint seams.

The Fix: Enable "High-Resolution 3D Rendering" in DraStic and ensure Frame Skip is set strictly to "None (0)".

Touchscreen Deadzone & Stylus Input Drift

The Issue: Capacitive finger touch can feel imprecise for quick mini-game stylus taps.

The Fix: Set Touch Screen Deadzone to "Minimal" and calibrate Stylus input to 1:1 hardware touch points in DraStic settings.

4. Touch Controls vs. Bluetooth Gamepads

Because Call Of Duty - Black Ops relies heavily on frame-precise input execution, multi-button simultaneous presses, and rapid directional motions (quarter-circle rolls, dragon-punch inputs), virtual on-screen controls can occasionally lead to misregistered inputs during intense combo exchanges. For casual arcade ladders, expanding the virtual button touch radii and configuring translucent 45% opacity overlays provides an accessible experience.

To truly dominate competitive matches, pairing a physical low-latency controller such as the Razer Kishi V2, GameSir G8 Galileo, or a Sony DualSense gamepad via USB-C or Bluetooth is strongly recommended. Physical microswitch D-pads and tactile face buttons guarantee zero dropped inputs, microsecond-accurate parries, and immediate combo execution.
